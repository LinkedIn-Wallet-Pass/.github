# LinkedInWallet

**Your LinkedIn profile, one tap away.**

[LinkedInWallet](https://linkedinwallet.com) makes exchanging LinkedIn profiles at conferences, meetups, and networking events faster. Create a digital networking pass, add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, and share your profile with a quick QR scan—without having to navigate through the LinkedIn app.

### How it works

1. Enter your name, LinkedIn profile, and a short bio at [linkedinwallet.com](https://linkedinwallet.com).
2. Generate your pass and add it to your phone's wallet.
3. Pull up the QR code to share your LinkedIn profile in person.

### Event Mode

Create a pass for a specific event with venue-aware relevance on supported Apple Wallet devices. Event Mode is designed to make your networking pass easier to access when you arrive, without searching for it.

### Built for real-world networking

- **Apple Wallet and Google Wallet** — a familiar, accessible place to keep your networking pass.
- **QR-first sharing** — a straightforward way to share your LinkedIn profile at busy events.
- **Event-aware passes** — optional event details, location relevance, and expiration.
- **5,000+ passes created** — and continuing to grow.
